## Offer and List:

* [ ] The email has one primary goal and one primary CTA.
* [ ] Audience segment matches the offer (do not mail everyone by default).
* [ ] Unsubscribe and physical address meet the rules for your region.
* [ ] From name and from address are recognizable.

## Content:

* [ ] Subject line is honest and specific.
* [ ] Preview text supports the subject, not a repeat of it.
* [ ] Body works in dark mode and with images off.
* [ ] Links go to the right UTM-tagged URLs.

## QA Send:

* [ ] Send tests to Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail if you can.
* [ ] Check spam words, broken merge tags, and leftover placeholders.
* [ ] Confirm the unsubscribe and preference links work.
* [ ] Confirm the landing page is live before the send.

## Send and Follow-Up:

* [ ] Schedule in the audience's timezone or agreed window.
* [ ] Watch bounce, complaint, and unsubscribe rates after send.
* [ ] Record open, click, and conversion results against the goal.
* [ ] Add learnings to the next campaign brief.
