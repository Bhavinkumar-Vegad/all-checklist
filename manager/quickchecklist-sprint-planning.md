## Purpose and People:

* [ ] Confirm the sprint goal in one sentence the team can repeat.
* [ ] Confirm the product owner, tech lead, and QA owner are in the room or async thread.
* [ ] Review carry-over work and why it did not finish.

## Backlog Readiness:

* [ ] Confirm upcoming stories have acceptance criteria.
* [ ] Split items that cannot finish in this sprint.
* [ ] Call out design, legal, or third-party blockers before committing.
* [ ] Estimate or size work using the team's agreed method.

## Capacity:

* [ ] Account for holidays, on-call, meetings, and support load.
* [ ] Leave slack for bugs and review, not only new features.
* [ ] Do not plan above the team's recent completed work without a reason.

## Commitment:

* [ ] Select a sprint backlog the team believes is achievable.
* [ ] Identify the first stories to start on day one.
* [ ] Capture risks and a mitigation owner.
* [ ] Publish the sprint goal and board link to stakeholders.

## After Planning:

* [ ] Update tickets with sprint, owner, and remaining open questions.
* [ ] Schedule refinement if more than a few stories are still vague.
* [ ] Confirm the demo date and who will present.
