## Before:

* [ ] Purpose is a decision, an alignment, or an info-share — pick one; if it is info-share, consider an email.
* [ ] Decision needed: options A/B/C, recommendation, and what you need from them (approve budget / cut scope / wait).
* [ ] Pre-read sent ≥ 24h with page numbers; if they will not read, you need a 3-slide version in the invite.
* [ ] Right people: the person with budget/legal authority is confirmed, not “please forward”.
* [ ] Parking lot is a real doc, not a promise to remember.

## In the Room:

* [ ] Start with the decision question, not a 15-minute project history.
* [ ] Data vs opinion is labelled; you do not argue anecdotes against a metric without saying so.
* [ ] Silent stakeholders are asked once; you still need a named decision.
* [ ] “Take it offline” has an owner and a date before you leave the item.
* [ ] You do not add scope because a senior person brainstormed unless they also add time/money.

## Decisions:

* [ ] Wording is specific: “Ship without SSO in v1; SSO in Q4; owner Priya” not “we’ll look at SSO”.
* [ ] Dissent is recorded if it is a governance meeting.
* [ ] Confidential items are marked; they do not go in the all-hands notes.

## After (same day):

* [ ] Notes: attendees, decisions, actions (owner, date), open questions.
* [ ] Board/RAID updated; otherwise the meeting did not happen.
* [ ] People who missed get the decisions, not a recap of discussion.
* [ ] If no decision, the blocker is named (missing person, missing number) and the next slot is booked.
