## Before the Meeting:

* [ ] Send a purpose, agenda, and desired decision in advance.
* [ ] Share pre-read material early enough to be read.
* [ ] Confirm the right decision-maker will attend.
* [ ] Prepare options, not only problems.

## During:

* [ ] Restate the goal in the first two minutes.
* [ ] Capture decisions, owners, and dates live.
* [ ] Park off-topic items instead of derailing the agenda.
* [ ] Confirm disagreements are about facts or priorities, then choose a path.
* [ ] End with a recap of what was decided and what is still open.

## After:

* [ ] Send notes the same day with decisions and action items.
* [ ] Update the project board or RAID log.
* [ ] Book the next checkpoint only if there is a reason.
* [ ] Follow up on overdue actions before the next meeting.
